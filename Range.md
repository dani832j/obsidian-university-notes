### Definition

The range of a function is the set of all output values

that the function can attain.

If

$$

f:A\to B,

$$

then the range is

$$

\{f(a):a\in A\}.

$$
___
### Example

Let

$$

f(x)=x^2,

\qquad x\in\mathbb{R}.

$$

Since squares are always non-negative,

$$

f(x)\geq 0.

$$

Therefore the range is

$$

[0,\infty).

$$

---

Let

$$

f(x)=\sin(x).

$$

The function can only take values between

$$

-1

$$

and

$$

1.

$$

Therefore the range is

$$

[-1,1].

$$

### Notes

The domain describes possible inputs.

The range describes possible outputs.

A codomain and a range are not necessarily the same thing.
